## 2026-10-31 Post-hoc: standardise rotation before scoring (user request) - step7_standardise.py, std/, std_analysis/
- Rule (written down before looking at the result): rotate each sequence so the start codon of its single intact family-F2 gene is at position 1 (forward gene: left end; reverse-strand gene: right end goes to the end of the sequence, so a reverse complement of a standard genome is left alone). 'Intact' = exactly one F2 gene, 0.8-1.25x the calibration median length (948 letters). Otherwise the sequence is left unchanged. Same rule for every damage type.
